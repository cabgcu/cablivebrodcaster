Show control

Canyon Night Live

Open each page on its device. iPads and phones can scan the codes. Everything plays from the laptop that runs the audience screen.

Before the show

  1. Laptop: connect the projector, drag a Chrome window onto it and open Audience screen. Click once to turn on sound and go full screen. Wait until the controller says Screen live, which means every slide and video has downloaded.
  2. iPad 1: scan Show control. In Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen makes it a full-screen app.
  3. iPad 2: scan Soundboard.
  4. Phones: scan Live camera, allow camera access, name the camera and tap Start camera.
  5. Keep the laptop and camera phones on the same Wi-Fi. Turn off Auto-Lock on camera phones, or plug them in. A locked phone stops its camera.
