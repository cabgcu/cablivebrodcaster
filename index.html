CANYON NIGHT LIVE

Show control. Open each page on its device. Phones and iPads can scan the codes.

Show-day checklist

  1. Laptop: connect the projector/TV, drag a Chrome window onto it and open Audience screen. Click once to turn on sound and go full screen. Wait for the controller to say Screen live: that means all slides and videos are downloaded to the laptop.
  2. iPad 1: scan the Show control code. Use Share → “Add to Home Screen” to get a full-screen app.
  3. iPad 2: scan the Soundboard code.
  4. Phones: scan the Live camera code, allow camera access, name the camera and tap Go live-ready.
  5. Put the laptop and camera phones on the same Wi-Fi. Camera video goes straight from phone to laptop over that Wi-Fi.
  6. Turn off Auto-Lock on the camera phones (Settings → Display & Brightness), or plug them in. If a phone locks, its camera feed stops.
