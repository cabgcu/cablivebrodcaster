CANYON NIGHT LIVE

Show control. Every device must be on the same Wi-Fi as this laptop.

Show-day checklist

  1. Laptop: connect the projector/TV, drag a Chrome window onto it and open Audience screen. Click once to turn on sound and go full screen. Audio plays from the laptop’s output.
  2. iPad 1: scan the Show control code. Use Share → “Add to Home Screen” to get a full-screen app.
  3. iPad 2: scan the Soundboard code.
  4. Phones: scan the Live camera code. The first time, Safari will warn about the certificate: tap Show Details → visit this website. Then allow camera access.
  5. Turn off Auto-Lock on the camera phones (Settings → Display & Brightness), or plug them in. If a phone locks, its camera feed stops.
